# Norwegian webshop candidates (2026-07-24 sweep)

Research pass across 7 category groups (parallel agents, web search + spot
JSON-LD/affiliate-network checks), followed by a second pass checking each
shop's own `robots.txt` and Terms of Service for scraping/automated-access
restrictions. ~218 real, currently-operating shops below. Not wired into
anything yet — this is a lead list, not a `SOURCES` config. **Compliance
notes are not legal advice** — they document what the public robots.txt/ToS
say, checked by an automated pass; verify directly before any real ingest.

## How to read the columns

**Ingest notes** (from pass 1) — three usable signals for HOW to pull data:

1. **Confirmed on Adtraction / Awin / Partner-ads / Tradedoubler** — apply
   for the program (see `ADTRACTION-COOKBOOK.md` for the Adtraction flow),
   then `adtractionSource()`-style feed parsing in `worker/sources.js`.
2. **Confirmed Product JSON-LD on product pages** — first-party `scrape`
   source, same as existing `scrape` config.
3. **Unknown** — nothing found in one search pass; check before onboarding.

**Scrape verdict** (from pass 2) — whether the shop's own terms allow it:

- **Prohibited** — ToS explicitly bans scraping/automated access/bots/crawlers.
- **Robots-blocked** — robots.txt disallows the relevant paths (product/
  category pages) or explicitly blocks scraper/AI bots by name.
- **Ambiguous** — only a generic "content may not be copied/reproduced"
  copyright clause, no explicit scraping/automation mention. Common and
  usually aimed at wholesale content republishing, not price extraction —
  but it's the ToS owner's call, not ours.
- **Silent** — no restriction found in either robots.txt or ToS.
- **Unknown** — couldn't reach/find the ToS page or robots.txt (bot-walled
  by Cloudflare/DataDome, JS-rendered, site down, etc).

Caveat: several checks used WebFetch, which converts pages to markdown and
can miss content behind JS rendering or bot-challenge walls — those are
called "Unknown", not "Silent". Re-check with a raw `curl`/browser before
relying on a "Silent" or "Ambiguous" verdict for anything at scale.

Every shop found in pass 1 gets a row below, including defunct/merged ones
(marked as such) — nothing is silently dropped. Truly out-of-scope
candidates the discovery agents ruled out before they became rows at all
(MPX.no closed to consumers, Gigantti is FI-only, Lefdal merged into
Elkjøp, Adams Matkasse merged into Godtlevert) aren't re-listed, since they
were never real candidates to begin with.
